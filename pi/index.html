The ESP32 pings the Pi's access point ~100×/s and streams the channel state of every reply. Motion = lag-1 autocorrelation of subcarrier amplitudes over 1 s (WiDetect). Breathing = 0.1–0.7 Hz peak of the top principal components over 30 s.
